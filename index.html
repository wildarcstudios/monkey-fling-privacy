Monkey Fling – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026
Publisher: Wild Arc Studios ("we", "us")
Contact: wildarcstudios.support@gmail.com

Monkey Fling is a single-player mixed-reality game for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S. It is for players aged 10 and over; players aged 10 to 12 use a parent-managed Meta account.

Short version

The game keeps your settings and progress on your headset only. It has no accounts, no online play, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send any information to us. We do not sell information.

What stays on your headset

  • Settings and progress: your choices (such as hand, tree size and aim help), level stars, scores and Daily Challenge streak.
  • Troubleshooting logs: the game keeps up to five short local session logs with controller activity, relative positions, game state, performance figures and errors. They are never sent automatically. If you choose to send one to support, it can contain those details.

This data lives in the app's private storage and is not included in Android backups. Uninstalling the game or using the reset options in Settings removes it.

Headset tracking and passthrough

The game uses controller and headset tracking to place the tree and respond to your movements, and shows your room through the headset's passthrough. Passthrough is handled by Meta's system software. The game does not access or record camera images, does not scan or store your room, uses no hand-tracking, microphone, location or contacts data, and does not transmit tracking data.

Ownership check

The store version uses the Meta Platform SDK once at start-up to ask whether the signed-in Meta account owns the game. The game does not read your name, profile, friends list or account identifier, and has no internet permission of its own. If ownership cannot be confirmed (for example you are offline), the game shows a message and closes. Meta handles the account and purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Age group

After the ownership check, the store version asks Meta once per session for the signed-in account's age group (child 10–12, teen 13–17 or adult 18+), as Meta requires for games made for all ages. Only the group name is kept, on your headset, so the game can use the last answer when you are offline. It is used only to keep age-appropriate behaviour (for example, children will not be offered online or social features if the game ever adds them). It is never sent to us or anyone else and never used for advertising. If Meta cannot answer, you can still play.

Children

Children aged 10 to 12 can play Monkey Fling with a parent-managed Meta account. The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children: no name, contact details, account identifier, photos, voice or location, and nothing is sent to us. A parent can delete everything the game stored by uninstalling it or using the reset options in Settings. Questions from parents are welcome at the contact address below.

Changes and questions

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before releasing the change. For privacy questions, contact wildarcstudios.support@gmail.com.
