Monkey Fling – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026
Publisher and data controller: Wild Arc Studios ("we", "us")
Contact: wildarcstudios.support@gmail.com

Monkey Fling is a single-player mixed-reality game for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S. It is for players aged 10 and over; players aged 10 to 12 use a parent-managed Meta account. This policy covers the Monkey Fling app and the data it collects, processes and stores.

Short version

The store version of Monkey Fling collects one piece of information about you: your Meta account's age group (child, teen or adult), which it receives from Meta through Meta's User Age Group platform feature. It uses it only to keep the game age-appropriate, and stores it on your headset. The game also stores your settings and progress on your headset. It has no accounts, no online play, no advertising and no analytics. We do not receive, sell or share any of this information. You can delete it at any time (see How to delete your data).

Information we collect and process

  • Age group (from Meta). What: the age group of the signed-in Meta account, one of child (10–12), teen (13–17) or adult (18+), received from Meta's User Age Group platform feature. It is not your birth date or exact age. How: after the ownership check, the store version asks Meta for it once per session and saves the group name in the app's private storage on your headset, so the last answer can be used when you are offline. Why: to provide the game in an age-appropriate way, as Meta requires for apps made for all ages; for example, children are not offered online or social features if the game ever adds them. It is never used for advertising or profiling, never sent to us and never shared with anyone. If Meta cannot answer, you can still play.
  • Meta account user ID (processed by Meta). To answer the ownership check and the age group request, Meta's platform uses the user ID of the signed-in Meta account. The game itself does not read, store or send your user ID, name, profile or friends list.
  • Ownership check. At start-up the store version asks Meta whether the signed-in account owns the game. Only a yes or no comes back; the game does not keep it. If ownership cannot be confirmed (for example you are offline), the game shows a message and closes.
  • Settings and progress. Your choices (such as hand, tree size and aim help), level stars, scores and Daily Challenge streak, stored on your headset so you can continue where you left off.
  • Troubleshooting logs. Up to five short local session logs with controller activity, relative positions, game state, performance figures and errors, kept on your headset to diagnose problems. They are never sent automatically. If you choose to send one to support, it can contain those details, and we use it only to answer your request.

Meta handles your Meta account, purchase and age information under its own privacy policy.

Where it is kept, sharing and retention

Everything above that the game stores stays in the app's private storage on your headset and is not included in Android backups. The game has no internet permission of its own; its only network use is Meta's Platform SDK for the ownership check and the age group request. We have no servers that hold your data, we do not sell or share it, and we use no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics. Stored data is kept until you delete it as described below. A log you send to support by email is kept only as long as needed to answer you, then deleted.

Headset tracking and passthrough

The game uses controller and headset tracking to place the tree and respond to your movements, and shows your room through the headset's passthrough. Passthrough is handled by Meta's system software. The game does not access or record camera images, does not scan or store your room, uses no hand-tracking, microphone, location or contacts data, and does not store or transmit tracking data (except inside a troubleshooting log you choose to send).

How to delete your data

  • In the game: Settings → System → Reset → Erase all data deletes your stored age group, settings, progress and scores.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling Monkey Fling deletes everything it stored on the headset, including the age group and the troubleshooting logs.
  • By email: send a deletion request to wildarcstudios.support@gmail.com with the subject "Monkey Fling data deletion request". We will delete any emails and logs you have sent us and confirm within 30 days, and help you remove the data stored on your headset.

To delete data that Meta holds about your Meta account (including your age information), use Meta's account settings or contact Meta as described in Meta's privacy policy.

Children

Children aged 10 to 12 can play Monkey Fling with a parent-managed Meta account. For every player, including children, the only information the game collects is the age group described above, which stays on the headset. The game does not collect names, contact details, account identifiers, photos, voice or location, and nothing is sent to us. A parent can delete everything the game stored using any of the options in How to delete your data. Questions from parents are welcome at the contact address below.

Changes and questions

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before releasing the change. For privacy questions or requests, contact wildarcstudios.support@gmail.com.
